What we do

Research Areas

Water Analytics for Variability & Extremes — four connected themes for understanding how water varies and when it turns extreme.

Hydrologic Analytics

Hydrological modeling, design rainfall, probable maximum flood, and parameter estimation in ungauged basins.

AI Forecasting

Hybrid machine learning with signal decomposition for rainfall, evapotranspiration, drought, and inflow forecasting.

Vision & Acoustic Sensing

Rainfall estimation from CCTV and infrared cameras, raindrop sounds, and low-cost IoT sensors.

Earth Observation

SAR/PolSAR flood detection, satellite and reanalysis data for extremes, and disaster monitoring.
