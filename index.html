Water Analytics for Variability with Earth Observation

지능형 수자원 연구실 (WAVE Lab)

We study water and its variability in space and time — from a single storm to basin-scale floods and droughts — through analytics that span hydrologic modeling, climate-extreme analysis, and AI-based forecasting, with Earth observation from satellites and radar.

What we do

Research Areas

Four connected themes for measuring, explaining, and forecasting how water varies, from ground sensors to satellites.

Hydrologic Analytics

Hydrological modeling, design rainfall, probable maximum flood, and parameter estimation in ungauged basins.

Learn more →

AI Forecasting

Hybrid machine learning with signal decomposition for rainfall, evapotranspiration, drought, and inflow forecasting.

Learn more →

Vision & Acoustic Sensing

Rainfall estimation from CCTV and infrared cameras, raindrop sounds, and low-cost IoT sensors.

Learn more →

Earth Observation

SAR/PolSAR flood detection, satellite and reanalysis data for extremes, and disaster monitoring.

Our logo

A drop of water, turned into a signal

  • The drop

    Water itself. Every measurement begins with a single raindrop.

  • The wave inside

    Water as nature moves it: broad, slow, and continuous.

  • The ripple that leaves

    The same wave, now a signal: measured, decomposed, and read with analytics and AI. It grows finer the closer we look.

  • The orbit

    Earth observation: a satellite circling above, watching water from space.

A wave is both water and a signal. We study one by reading the other.
