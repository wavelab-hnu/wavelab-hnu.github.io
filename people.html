Jinwook Lee, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor

Prof. Lee's research spans hydrological modeling, climate extremes, AI-based forecasting, rainfall estimation using computer vision and acoustics, and flood detection from SAR satellite data. Before joining Hannam University in 2026, he was a postdoctoral associate at the University of Hawaii at Manoa working on NSF/NASA- and USDA-funded projects, and a postdoctoral researcher at Chung-Ang University.
